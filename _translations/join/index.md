## Step 2: Receive Your Group Invitation

Once you have added the assistant, you will be invited to the Qifa Talk WeChat group. **You do not need to send another message.** Event announcements and registration details are shared in the group.

## Step 3: Explore Events and Sign Up

Events are usually free. We generally post event introductions and sign-up lists in the WeChat group on **Mondays at 9 PM**. Check each announcement for the exact time, location, and available places.

[View Upcoming Events →]({{ '/upcoming/' | locale_url }})

## What Can You Join?

The format varies by topic. Our regular activities include:

* **Debates**: Structured discussions with opposing sides.
* **Talks and Sharing**: Member-led presentations and open conversation.
* **Movie Nights**: Screenings followed by in-depth discussion.
* **Outdoor Gatherings**: Relaxed conversation while walking together.
